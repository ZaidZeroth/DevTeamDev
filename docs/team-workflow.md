# Team workflow

This workflow keeps studio-wide improvement work in DevTeamDev and game-specific work in the relevant game's repository.

## Choose the right place

- Use an issue for a concrete task, decision, question with an owner, or improvement that needs follow-up.
- Use a GitHub Discussion for an open-ended conversation when Discussions are enabled and available.
- Keep game-specific implementation, design, and playtesting in the game's own repository.
- Do not duplicate the same policy or task across several posts. Link related records and maintain one clear source of truth.

## Make work actionable

For each issue that needs action, state the goal, context, expected outcome, owner, and next review point. Keep discussion focused and public-safe. Record a decision and its rationale when the team reaches one; assign follow-up work to the appropriate role.

## Review and contribute

Staff review current issues and discussions on their agreed cadence. For each item, decide whether it is relevant to the role and whether a useful response or action is due. Avoid placeholder, repetitive, or unrelated comments. Track unresolved questions and expected owners.

## Screen public changes

- Staff other than the Broadcaster are read-only on GitHub.
- Submit the exact full proposed content and action through the approved broadcast workflow.
- Every broadcast must state its intended repository. For a GitHub action, use one exact `owner/repo` destination and identify the target issue, discussion, or file.
- Broadcaster screens each complete action. Approval of the exact screened action authorizes only that publication on the named repository, without a separate owner approval step.
- Reject unclear or unsafe requests with clear resubmission guidance; do not leave them pending. A clarified resubmission must be a new immutable request linked to the earlier request. Approval does not authorize weakening a prime directive or protected role guarantee; that requires explicit studio-owner permission.
- Never include private ARG text, credentials, sensitive or personal information, or details withheld from public discussion.

## Change role guidance carefully

Discuss substantive changes with the role owner before implementation. Check whether a wording or structure change alters behavior or weakens a protected guarantee. The studio owner alone may authorize weakening a prime directive. Keep the approved rationale and resulting action traceable.

## Learn from outcomes

Use concrete outcomes, playtest evidence, and respectful feedback to improve both the game and the team's process. Recognize specific completed contributions honestly, name remaining uncertainty, and keep workload sustainable.
